# Image credits for the Minstrel design mockups

The "Minstrel Mono" design canvas uses 15 sample photos. The canvas is at
https://claude.ai/artifact/FeUxZspMaFVnnhML8H2uf4.
The photos come from Openverse. Each photo is cropped, and its colour is a little softer.

> **Caution:** 13 photos use the CC BY 2.0 license. This license requires a credit.
> Before launch, do one of these two things:
>
> 1. Keep a credit line for each photo on the page that shows it. The Home footer has one now.
> 2. Replace the photos with your own images or with the cover art that the music provider returns.

The CC0 photos do not require a credit, but a credit is good practice.
